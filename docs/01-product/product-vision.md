# VisitsPakistan.com --- Product Vision

## Purpose

VisitsPakistan.com is a commercial travel discovery, planning and
local-experience marketplace for Pakistan. It is designed to acquire
travelers through organic search, AI discovery and social channels, help
them understand and plan Pakistan trips, and connect high-intent
visitors with trusted local tourism suppliers.

## Product Promise

**Discover Pakistan. Experience it locally.**

## Core Journey

**Inspire → Discover → Research → Plan → Compare → Connect / Book**

## Product Pillars

1.  **Discover** --- destinations, attractions, things to do,
    experiences, neighbourhoods, food, culture, nature, adventure and
    stories.
2.  **Plan** --- routes, weather, best time to visit, itineraries, maps,
    visa and practical travel information.
3.  **Experience** --- tours, activities, packages, guides, hotels,
    restaurants and local experiences.
4.  **Connect** --- tour operators, DMCs, guides, transport providers
    and other verified local partners.

## Primary Users

International and domestic travelers; local tourism partners; content
contributors; editors; administrators and moderators.

## Strategic Assets

1.  Organic and AI-discovery audience.
2.  A structured Pakistan Travel Knowledge Graph.
3.  A verified local supplier network.

## MVP Scope

Destinations, attractions, things to do, experiences, food, travel
guides, routes, itineraries, unified search, partner directory,
verification, tours/packages, contact/WhatsApp conversion, analytics and
SEO/GEO foundations.

## Post-MVP

Request-a-Quote marketplace, partner dashboard, maps, events,
neighbourhoods, personalisation, grounded AI trip planner, affiliates
and later transactional booking.

## Product Principles

-   Structured travel entities are first-class domain objects, not only
    CMS pages.
-   Verification cannot be purchased.
-   Sponsored placement must be clearly identified and must not alter
    organic relevance.
-   AI must be grounded in approved VisitsPakistan data.
-   Mobile performance and crawlability are launch requirements.
-   Build commerce in stages: discovery → lead generation → booking.
