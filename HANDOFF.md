`GET /api/v1/destinations` supports ancestor region, exact interest, season and bounded pagination. `GET /api/v1/destinations/{slug}` composes canonical geography, published editorial snapshots, approved attractions/experiences/restaurant places, sources and verification dates. Destinations owns discovery profiles referencing existing CITY or DESTINATION GeoEntity UUIDs; Geography owns identity, hierarchy and PostGIS coordinates. Public reads require published, verified records backed by active non-fixture sources. Existing `/v1` routes remain compatibility aliases. Canonical DTOs expose sourced summary, immediate parent ID, lifecycle dates and coordinates/GeoJSON derived from PostGIS. Detail adds eligible immediate parent, up to 48 direct children and up to six sibling destinations. Links are emitted only when a child has an eligible destination profile. Composition uses a repeatable-read transaction. Structured routes/itineraries/travel products remain empty because their canonical owner modules are unimplemented; published food/route/itinerary guides remain separate editorial content. No partner marketplace was added.

Next.js `/destinations` and `/destinations/[slug]` provide responsive cards, GET filters, pagination, detail sections, source attribution, canonical/OpenGraph metadata, breadcrumbs and JSON-LD. Server rendering uses no-store for immediate withdrawal. Filter URLs are noindexed and canonicalized to the directory. Quick answer, overview, why visit, best time, travel tips and FAQ are revision-owned presentation fields; optional legacy snapshots remain supported. Sitemap.xml includes eligible English destination URLs with actual update dates; robots.txt and llms.txt link public discovery. Sitemap sharding/bulk projection is required before 50000 URLs. Missing destinations return real HTTP 404; upstream failures show an availability error. Directory loading is scoped to a route group so it cannot cause detail soft 404s; card navigation has an accessible pending indicator.

The CMS retains eight editorial families, immutable published snapshots, draft/review/approved/published workflow, author/reviewer separation, RBAC staff sessions, audit/outbox, theme/template management and local WebP media. Canonical entities stay authoritative outside the CMS. Passwords use Argon2id and staff login uses TOTP; same-origin BFF cookies and origin checks protect writes. Bootstrap credentials remain ignored. Outbox delivery and production staff provisioning/recovery remain future work. MIT Tabler styling/icons and Majestic Indus tokens use self-hosted fonts; the screenshot-derived logo still needs the approved original vector.
